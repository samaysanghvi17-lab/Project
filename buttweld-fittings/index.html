Frequently asked questions

What sizes of buttweld fittings do you make?

Seamless fittings up to 24″ and welded fittings up to 72″, in standard schedules or to match your pipe wall.

Which standards do your buttweld fittings follow?

ASME B16.9 and B16.28, MSS SP-43 for light-wall stainless, and EN 10253, with bevels to ASME B16.25 and marking to MSS SP-25.

Do you ship buttweld fittings to the USA and Canada?

Yes. See our USA and Canada export pages for documents, ports and delivery terms.
