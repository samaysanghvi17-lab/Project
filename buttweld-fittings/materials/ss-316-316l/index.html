About SS 316 / 316L

Grade 316 adds 2–3% molybdenum to the 304 chemistry, which improves resistance to pitting and crevice corrosion in chloride environments. 316L has a lower carbon limit (0.030% max) for welded service. Dual-certified 316/316L is common.

SS 316 / 316L buttweld fittings

Neo Impex supplies 316/316L buttweld elbows, tees, reducers, caps, stub ends, crosses, return bends and bends, seamless up to 24″ and welded up to 72″, with 100% radiography on welded seams and EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

Typical uses

  • Marine and coastal installations
  • Pharmaceutical and food-grade lines
  • Chemical processing
  • Water and wastewater with chlorides

Limits and selection notes

For very high chloride, seawater at temperature, or sour service, step up to duplex or super duplex.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between 316 and 316L?

The carbon limit: 0.08% for 316 and 0.030% for 316L. The lower carbon gives better resistance to sensitization in welded parts. Dual-certified 316/316L meets both.

Is 316L suitable for seawater?

It resists chloride better than 304 but can still pit in warm seawater. Duplex or super duplex is normally specified for seawater service.
