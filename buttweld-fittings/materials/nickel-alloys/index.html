Nickel and Special Alloys

About Nickel and Special Alloys

Nickel alloys resist severe corrosion, high temperature and aggressive chemicals where stainless steel is not enough. Neo Impex works with Inconel, Incoloy, Monel, Hastelloy and Alloy 20.

Nickel and Special Alloys buttweld fittings

Neo Impex supplies nickel-alloy buttweld fittings to ASTM B366 in the sizes and forms your project needs. State the alloy by name and UNS number, with the standard and test requirements.

Typical uses

  • Aggressive acid service (Alloy 20, Hastelloy)
  • Seawater and brine (Monel, Inconel)
  • High-temperature and sour environments
  • Chemical and offshore processing

Limits and selection notes

Prices follow nickel and molybdenum markets and lead times are longer than for stainless. Request a quote per project with the exact UNS grade.

Frequently asked questions

How do I order nickel-alloy fittings?

State the alloy name and UNS number, the fitting type and size, wall thickness, standard (ASTM B366) and any special tests.

Why do nickel alloys cost more?

Nickel and molybdenum are costly and the alloys are harder to form and weld. Pricing changes with metal markets, so quotes are valid for a short period.
