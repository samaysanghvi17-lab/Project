About Carbon Steel (A234 WPB / A105)

Carbon steel is the workhorse for oil, gas, water and steam pipelines. ASTM A234 WPB covers buttweld fittings and ASTM A105 covers forged flanges. They are strong, easy to weld and cost-effective, but need coatings or linings against corrosion.

Carbon Steel buttweld fittings

Neo Impex keeps a large stock of carbon steel buttweld elbows and common fittings for fast dispatch. Every piece is marked with grade, size, schedule and heat number. Welded fittings to 72″ are available, with 100% radiography on welded seams.

Typical uses

  • Oil and gas pipelines
  • Water and steam lines
  • General process piping
  • Pipe spools and fabricated skids

Limits and selection notes

For service colder than about −29 °C (−20 °F), choose impact-tested low-temperature carbon steel (A350 LF2 flanges, A420 WPL6 fittings).

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between WPB and WPC?

Both are carbon steel fitting grades. WPC has higher minimum tensile and yield strength than WPB. WPB is the usual choice.

Can carbon steel fittings be supplied for sour service?

Yes, with NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 hardness and testing requirements stated in the order.
