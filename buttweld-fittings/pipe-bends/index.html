Overview

A pipe bend gives a gentler change of direction than a standard elbow. The radius is quoted as a multiple of the pipe diameter: a 5D bend has a centerline radius of five times the nominal diameter. Larger radii mean lower pressure drop and easier passage for in-line inspection tools.

Neo Impex makes 3D, 5D and 10D bends, induction (hot) bends and piggable bends up to 24″ and radii to R = 22D in our own bending shop. Bends ship with heat-traceable marking and EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

Features

  • Long radii for low pressure drop
  • Piggable bends for pipeline inspection tools
  • Made in our own bending shop
  • Custom angles and tangents to your drawing

Typical applications

  • Oil, gas and product pipelines
  • Pipeline launchers and receivers
  • Compact process lines needing gentle bends
  • Heat exchanger and heater piping

Frequently asked questions

What does 5D mean?

The centerline bend radius equals five times the nominal pipe diameter. A 3D bend is tighter, a 10D bend is gentler.

What is a piggable bend?

A bend with a radius large enough for in-line inspection and cleaning tools (pigs) to pass. We make piggable bends up to 24″ and to R = 22D.

Can you bend to my drawing?

Yes. Send the drawing with angle, radius, tangents, material and standard, and we will quote.
