Overview

A buttweld cap closes the end of a pipe permanently. It is welded on, has a domed (ellipsoidal) head, and is designed to carry the same pressure as the pipe it closes.

Neo Impex makes caps in stainless, duplex, carbon, alloy and nickel alloys, seamless up to 24″ and welded up to 72″, with heat-traceable marking and EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

Features

  • Domed head for strength and smooth stress distribution
  • Same wall as the pipe for equal pressure rating
  • Dimensions to ASME B16.9
  • Heat-traceable marking

Typical applications

  • Line ends and future tie-ins
  • Pressure vessel and tank nozzles
  • Pipe spools and headers
  • Pigging and test headers

Available materials

  • SS 304 / 304L
  • SS 316 / 316L
  • Duplex 2205 (F51 / F60)
  • Super Duplex 2507 (F53 / F55)
  • Carbon Steel (A234 WPB / A105)
  • Low-Temp Carbon Steel (A420 WPL6 / A350 LF2)
  • Alloy Steel Chrome-Moly (F11 / F22 / F91)
  • Nickel and Special Alloys

Frequently asked questions

Should I use a cap or a blind flange?

A cap is welded on and is permanent. A blind flange is bolted so the line can be reopened. Use a cap for a final end and a blind flange where you may need access.

Can caps be supplied in large sizes?

Yes. Caps are seamless up to 24″ and welded from plate up to 72″.
