Buttweld Tees: Equal and Reducing

Equal and reducing tees for branch lines. Manufactured in India and exported to the USA and Canada with EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

ASME B16.9Equal and reducingWelded to 72″

Overview

A buttweld tee creates a 90° branch off a main run. Equal tees have the same size on all three ends; reducing tees have a smaller branch outlet. Neo Impex makes tees to ASME B16.9 in stainless, duplex, carbon, alloy and nickel alloys.

Seamless tees are hot-formed from pipe up to 24″. Larger tees are fabricated from plate up to 72″ and radiographed along the weld. Every tee carries heat-number marking and an EN 10204 3.1 certificate.

Features

  • Equal and reducing outlets
  • Hot-formed seamless tees up to 24″
  • Plate-fabricated tees up to 72″ with radiography
  • Dimensions to ASME B16.9
  • Heat-traceable marking

Typical applications

  • Branch connections on process lines
  • Pipeline headers and manifolds
  • Water and wastewater distribution
  • Fabricated pipe spools and skids

Frequently asked questions

What is a reducing tee?

A tee whose branch is smaller than the run, for example 8″ × 8″ × 4″. Sizes are quoted run × run × branch.

How are large-diameter tees made?

Above 24″ they are fabricated from plate and welded. Welds are 100% radiographed.

How do I specify a tee?

Give nominal size (run × branch), schedule or wall on each end, material grade and standard, for example: 8″ × 4″ SCH 40S, ASTM A403 WP316L, ASME B16.9.
