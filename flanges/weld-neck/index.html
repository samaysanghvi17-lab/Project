Overview

A weld neck flange has a long tapered hub that is butt-welded to the pipe. The hub carries stress smoothly from the pipe into the flange, so weld neck flanges are the usual choice for high pressure, high temperature, cyclic and critical service.

Neo Impex forges weld neck flanges from ½″ to 60″ in Class 150 to 2500 (size dependent), with raised face, ring type joint and other facings. The bore is machined to match your pipe schedule.

Features

  • Hub transfers stress smoothly into the pipe
  • Suits high-pressure and cyclic service
  • Bore matched to pipe schedule for smooth flow
  • Radiography-friendly butt weld joint

Typical applications

  • Refinery and petrochemical piping
  • Oil and gas pipelines
  • Power plant steam lines
  • High-pressure chemical service

Frequently asked questions

What does WNRF mean?

Weld neck, raised face. WNRTJ means weld neck with a ring type joint facing.

Why must the bore match the pipe?

A step between the flange bore and the pipe bore disturbs flow and can cause erosion. Specify the pipe schedule so we machine the bore to match.

Weld neck or slip-on?

Choose weld neck for high pressure, temperature swings or critical lines. Slip-on is lower cost for moderate service. Our blog compares them in detail.
