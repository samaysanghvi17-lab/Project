Socket Weld Flanges (SWRF)

Small-bore, high-pressure lines. Manufactured in India and exported to the USA and Canada with EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

Overview

A socket weld flange has a recess (socket) into which the pipe is inserted and fillet-welded. It is used on small-bore, high-pressure lines, where its smooth bore and easy fit-up are an advantage.

Neo Impex forges socket weld flanges to ASME B16.5 in stainless, duplex, carbon, alloy and nickel alloys, with heat-number traceability and EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

Features

  • Suits small-bore, high-pressure lines
  • Simple fit-up with a single fillet weld
  • Smooth bore
  • Heat-number traceability

Typical applications

  • Instrument and sampling lines
  • Hydraulic and lube oil systems
  • High-pressure small-bore process piping

Frequently asked questions

Why is there a gap when welding a socket weld flange?

The pipe is inserted to the bottom of the socket and then pulled back about 1.6 mm (1/16″) before welding, to allow for thermal expansion during welding.

What size range is typical?

Socket weld flanges are used on small bore, typically up to NPS 3. Larger sizes use weld neck or slip-on flanges.
