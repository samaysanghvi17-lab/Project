Frequently asked questions

What sizes and classes of flanges do you make?

Flanges from ½″ to 60″ in Class 150 to 2500, depending on size and the applicable standard (PN6 to PN100 for EN flanges).

Which standards do your flanges follow?

ASME B16.5 for NPS ½–24 and ASME B16.47 for NPS 26–60, plus EN 1092-1, BS 4504, BS 10 and DIN.

What facings can you supply?

Raised face, ring type joint, flat face, tongue and groove, and male and female facings.
