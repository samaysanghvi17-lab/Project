About Alloy Steel Chrome-Moly (F11 / F22 / F91)

Chrome-moly (chromium-molybdenum) steels keep their strength at high temperature and resist hydrogen and oxidizing attack. Grades F11, F22 and F91 are the usual choices for refinery and power service.

Alloy Steel Chrome-Moly flanges

Neo Impex forges F11, F22 and F91 flanges from ½″ to 60″ in weld neck, slip-on, blind and RTJ forms, to ASME B16.5 and B16.47, with heat-treatment records.

Typical uses

  • Refinery hydrogen and hydrocracking units
  • Power plant main steam and hot reheat
  • Petrochemical furnaces and heaters

Limits and selection notes

Chrome-moly needs controlled heat treatment and welding, with preheat and post-weld heat treatment (PWHT) as the code requires. F91 is especially sensitive to heat treatment.

Frequently asked questions

What are F11, F22 and F91 used for?

Elevated-temperature piping in refineries and power plants. F91 is the creep-strength-enhanced grade for the hottest service.

What do I need to specify when ordering?

Grade and class (for example F22 Class 3), size and rating, facing, standard and test requirements.
