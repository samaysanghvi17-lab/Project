About Super Duplex 2507 (F53 / F55)

Super duplex stainless steels such as 2507 have higher chromium, molybdenum and nitrogen than 2205, giving a pitting resistance equivalent (PREN) of about 40 or more. They combine very high strength with excellent resistance to seawater and chloride attack.

Super Duplex 2507 flanges

Neo Impex forges super duplex F53 and F55 flanges from ½″ to 60″ to ASME B16.5 and B16.47, with NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 documentation available.

Typical uses

  • Offshore and subsea piping
  • Seawater cooling and firewater
  • Desalination
  • High-chloride chemical service

Limits and selection notes

Welding and heat treatment must be tightly controlled to keep the phase balance. Typical upper service temperature is about 315 °C (600 °F).

Frequently asked questions

Which is better for seawater, F53 or F55?

Both are super duplex and widely used offshore. F53 is UNS S32750 (2507) and F55 is UNS S32760. Follow your project material specification.

Do you supply super duplex flanges with NACE certificates?

Yes. State MR0175 / ISO 15156 and any hardness limits when you order.
