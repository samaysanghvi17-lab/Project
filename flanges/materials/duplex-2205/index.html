Duplex 2205

About Duplex 2205 (F51 / F60)

Duplex 2205 has a mixed ferritic and austenitic structure, with about 22% chromium, 5% nickel and 3% molybdenum. Its yield strength is roughly twice that of 304/316, and it resists chloride stress corrosion cracking much better.

Duplex 2205 flanges

Neo Impex forges duplex F51 and F60 flanges from ½″ to 60″ to ASME B16.5 and B16.47, with NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 documentation available for sour service.

Typical uses

  • Oil and gas topsides and process
  • Desalination plants
  • Chemical processing
  • Pulp and paper

Limits and selection notes

Duplex is usually limited to about 315 °C (600 °F) because prolonged exposure at higher temperatures can embrittle it. Check your design code.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between F51 and F60?

Both are 2205 duplex. F60 (UNS S32205) is the later, tighter-composition version with slightly higher minimum tensile strength. Many suppliers dual-certify F51/F60.

Can duplex flanges be supplied for sour service?

Yes, with NACE MR0175 / ISO 15156 requirements stated in the order. Tell us the hardness limit and tests you need.
