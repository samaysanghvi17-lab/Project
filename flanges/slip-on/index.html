Slip-On Flanges (SORF)

SORF for low-pressure lines where cost and fit-up matter. Manufactured in India and exported to the USA and Canada with EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

Overview

A slip-on flange slides over the pipe and is welded inside and outside with two fillet welds. It costs less than a weld neck flange and is easier to align, which makes it popular for low-pressure, non-critical lines.

Neo Impex forges slip-on flanges to ASME B16.5 in stainless, duplex, carbon, alloy and nickel alloys, with raised face and other facings, supplied with heat-number traceability and EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

Features

  • Lower cost than weld neck
  • Easy alignment and fit-up
  • Raised face and other facings
  • Heat-number traceability

Typical applications

  • Water and utility lines
  • Low-pressure process piping
  • Fire protection and cooling water
  • Non-critical chemical lines

Frequently asked questions

What does SORF mean?

Slip-on, raised face.

When is a slip-on flange not suitable?

Avoid slip-on flanges for severe cyclic loading or high pressure and temperature, where a weld neck flange has better fatigue performance. Your piping code and line class decide.
