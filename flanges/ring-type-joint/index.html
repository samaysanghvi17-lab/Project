Ring Type Joint (RTJ) Flanges

Ring joint grooves for Class 600–2500 oil and gas. Manufactured in India and exported to the USA and Canada with EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

ASME B16.5Class 600–2500Ring groove
Request a quote

Overview

A ring type joint flange has a machined groove on its face that holds a metal ring gasket. When the flanges are bolted together the ring seals metal-to-metal, which suits high pressure and temperature.

Neo Impex forges RTJ flanges from ½″ to 24″ to ASME B16.5, most often in Class 600 to 2500 for oil and gas, in carbon, alloy, stainless, duplex and nickel alloys.

Features

  • Metal-to-metal sealing for high pressure
  • Grooves machined to ring gasket dimensions
  • Weld neck and blind RTJ available

Typical applications

  • Wellhead and manifold piping
  • High-pressure pipelines
  • Refinery high-pressure units

Available materials

  • SS 304 / 304L
  • SS 316 / 316L
  • Duplex 2205 (F51 / F60)
  • Super Duplex 2507 (F53 / F55)
  • Carbon Steel (A234 WPB / A105)
  • Low-Temp Carbon Steel (A420 WPL6 / A350 LF2)
  • Alloy Steel Chrome-Moly (F11 / F22 / F91)
  • Nickel and Special Alloys
