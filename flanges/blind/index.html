Blind Flanges

Close pipe ends, valves and vessel nozzles. Manufactured in India and exported to the USA and Canada with EN 10204 3.1 certificates.

ASME B16.5 · B16.47½″ to 60″Class 150–2500
Request a quote

Overview

A blind flange is a solid disc with bolt holes and no bore. It closes the end of a pipe, a valve or a vessel nozzle, and can be unbolted when you need access.

Neo Impex forges blind flanges from ½″ to 60″ in Class 150 to 2500 (size dependent), in stainless, duplex, carbon, alloy and nickel alloys, with raised face, ring type joint and other facings.

Features

  • Solid forged disc, no bore
  • Sizes to 60″ for large vessels
  • Raised face, RTJ and other facings
  • Heat-number traceability

Typical applications

  • Line ends and future tie-ins
  • Vessel and tank manways and nozzles
  • Pressure test closures
  • Valve and equipment isolation

Available materials

  • SS 304 / 304L
  • SS 316 / 316L
  • Duplex 2205 (F51 / F60)
  • Super Duplex 2507 (F53 / F55)
  • Carbon Steel (A234 WPB / A105)
  • Low-Temp Carbon Steel (A420 WPL6 / A350 LF2)
  • Alloy Steel Chrome-Moly (F11 / F22 / F91)
  • Nickel and Special Alloys

Frequently asked questions

What is a spectacle blind?

A figure-eight plate with one solid and one open disc, used for positive isolation (ASME B16.48). Send us your specification for a quote.

Blind flange or cap?

A blind flange can be reopened; a buttweld cap is welded on permanently.
