Seamless vs Welded Buttweld Fittings: Which Should You Choose?

How seamless and welded buttweld fittings are made, where each is used, and what to ask your supplier about size limits, radiography and certificates.

By Neo Impex Stainless Pvt. Ltd. · Published 2026-11-22

Both seamless and welded buttweld fittings are made to ASME B16.9 and fit the same pipe. The difference is how they are made, and that affects size range, cost and what you should ask for in the purchase order.

How seamless fittings are made

Seamless fittings are formed from a length of seamless pipe or a forged billet. The pipe is heated and pushed over a die (hot forming) so it takes the shape of an elbow, tee or reducer. There is no longitudinal weld seam in the body.

Neo Impex makes seamless fittings up to 24″.

How welded fittings are made

Welded fittings are made from plate or welded pipe, formed into shape and joined with one or more welds. This is how most large-diameter fittings are made, because seamless pipe and forming equipment are limited at big sizes.

Neo Impex makes welded fittings up to 72″ and radiographs 100% of the welded seams.

Which should you choose?

Use seamless where your specification requires it or for sizes up to 24″ where lead time and cost are similar. Use welded for large diameters, for heavy walls in special alloys, and where the design allows welded fittings with full radiography.

Many piping classes allow both. Check your line class and code, because the design factors for welded fittings depend on the extent of examination.

What to ask your supplier

Ask for the heat number on every piece, the EN 10204 3.1 certificate, and confirmation of radiography on welded seams. For stainless steels, ask for the heat treatment (solution annealing) record.

State seamless or welded clearly in the purchase order, together with the size, schedule, grade and standard.
