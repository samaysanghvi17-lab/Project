About Neo Impex

We make buttweld fittings and forged flanges at our works in Vadodara, India, and export them to buyers in the USA, Canada and other markets.

What we do

Neo Impex Stainless Pvt. Ltd. manufactures buttweld pipe fittings and bends, and forged flanges, in stainless steel, duplex, carbon, alloy and nickel alloys. Fittings are seamless up to 24″ and welded up to 72″. Flanges run from ½″ to 60″ in Class 150 to 2500, depending on size.

How we work

Forging, forming, bending, machining and testing are done at our works in Vadodara. Every piece is heat traceable and ships with an EN 10204 3.1 certificate. Welded seams are 100% radiographed.

Quality and approvals

Our quality system is certified to ISO 9001:2015. We hold a PED 2014/68/EU material manufacturer approval and an IBR workshop approval for pipe fittings, and we are registered as a Small manufacturing enterprise on the Udyam portal. See each certificate and its scope.

Markets

We supply distributors, fabricators and contractors in the USA and Canada, and have supplied customers in more than 25 countries. See our USA and Canada pages for documents, ports and delivery terms.
