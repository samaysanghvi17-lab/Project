Buttweld Fittings Data Sheet

Standards, sizes, materials, manufacturing, marking and documents for Neo Impex buttweld fittings and bends.

Request a signed copy

1. Scope and standards

ProductsElbows (45°, 90°, 180°), tees, reducers, caps, stub ends, crosses, return bends, pipe bends
StandardsASME B16.9 · ASME B16.28 · MSS SP-43 · EN 10253 · ASME B16.49 (induction bends)
End preparationBevelled for butt welding (ASME B16.25)
MarkingMSS SP-25: grade, size, schedule, heat number

2. Sizes and wall thickness

SeamlessUp to 24″ (NPS)
WeldedUp to 72″ (NPS)
Wall thicknessSch 5S to XXS, or to match your pipe
Bends3D, 5D, 10D, induction and piggable to R = 22D, up to 24″

4. Manufacturing and testing

Seamless fittingsHot-formed from seamless pipe
Welded fittingsFabricated from plate; 100% radiography on welded seams
Heat treatmentAs required by the material, for example solution annealing for austenitic stainless steels
Additional testsSuch as PMI, hydrostatic or impact tests, can be specified in the purchase order

5. Documents and delivery

CertificatesEN 10204 3.1 mill test certificate with heat numbers
InspectionThird-party inspection can be arranged on request
Shipping documentsCommercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading, certificate of origin
TermsFOB, CIF or DAP
