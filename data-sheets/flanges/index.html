Forged Flanges Data Sheet

Standards, types, sizes, classes, facings, materials, marking and documents for Neo Impex forged flanges.

Request a signed copy

1. Scope and standards

StandardsASME B16.5 (NPS ½–24) · ASME B16.47 Series A and B (NPS 26–60) · EN 1092-1 · BS 4504 · BS 10 · DIN
Sizes½″ to 60″
Pressure classClass 150 to 2500 (size dependent) · PN6 to PN100
FacingsRaised face, ring type joint, flat face, tongue and groove, male and female
MarkingManufacturer, material, class, standard, size, heat number

2. Flange types

TypeUse
Weld Neck FlangesWNRF and RTJ for high-pressure and critical service
Slip-On FlangesSORF for low-pressure lines where cost and fit-up matter
Blind FlangesClose pipe ends, valves and vessel nozzles
Socket Weld FlangesSmall-bore, high-pressure lines
Threaded FlangesNPT screwed flanges where welding isn’t allowed
Lap Joint FlangesLoose flange for lines that are often dismantled
Ring Type Joint (RTJ) FlangesRing joint grooves for Class 600–2500 oil and gas
Long Weld Neck FlangesExtended hubs for vessels and column nozzles
